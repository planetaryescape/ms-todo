## Answer

**Links behave like steps for `If-Match`, but can't have a field cleared; steps can't be reordered.** Confidence: high (each row observed directly).

Every probe step and the link were deleted after (204 each); the task was back to no steps and no link.

## Conclusion

- A link needs `applicationName`: ms-todo sends `ms-todo` when none is given.
- Send the parent task's etag as `If-Match` on step **and** link PATCH and DELETE; a 412 means the task changed since it was read.
- A link's PATCH sends only what changes; a field can't be cleared, so undoing an edit that set a field the link didn't have deletes the link and adds it back as it was.
- **Steps have no order to set**: they stay in the order they were added, and nothing (a field, `$orderby`) changes it. `steps order` and the TUI's `J`/`K` are left out. Reordering by deleting and re-adding every step was rejected: each re-add is a create with nothing to attribute it by (D-028), so a lost answer could duplicate or lose steps.
