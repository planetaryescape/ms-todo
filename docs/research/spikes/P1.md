## Conclusion

- Default page is 50 for tasks (list and delta). Always follow `@odata.nextLink` until it's absent (list) or a `@odata.deltaLink` appears (delta).
- Never infer "last page" from a short or empty page: delta regularly ends with an empty page that carries the deltaLink, and lists/delta returned a 6-item page mid-stream.
- `Prefer: odata.maxpagesize` works on both list and delta but must be sent on **every** page request (it isn't carried in nextLink).
  `maxpagesize=200` is a reasonable choice for initial sync (fewer round trips); nothing broke at 144 items.
