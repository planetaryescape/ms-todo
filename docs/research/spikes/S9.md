# S9: throttling

Run: 2026-09-24 14:22–14:24 UTC. All requests `GET /me/todo/lists/{L}/tasks/{S1 task}` on the throwaway list. Auth header redacted.
A background prober (4–6 sequential delta GETs once a minute, see S4) was running at the same time.

## Answer

**The effective concurrency limit is 4 in-flight requests per app+mailbox, as documented for Outlook, and it applies to To Do.**
- Burst 1: 4 parallel GETs → 4×200, max latency 0.33 s.
- Burst 2: 8 parallel GETs → **6×200, 2×429** (`Retry-After: 7` and `8`). Bursting stopped here (rule: stop on first 429); burst 3 not run.
- After waiting 10 s, a single GET → 200.
- The same limit hits inside `$batch` (S10): a 7-request parallel batch had 1×429; 20-request parallel batches had 11×429 and similar, `Retry-After` 2–10 s.
- Sequential traffic never throttled: 120 POSTs in 6 sequential batches in 24 s, plus ~150 sequential GETs over the session, zero 429s.

429 body: `{"error":{"code":"activityLimitReached","message":"The app or user has been throttled.","innerError":{"code":"ApplicationThrottled",…}}}`.

**Headers:** only `Retry-After` (integer seconds). **No `x-ms-throttle-limit-percentage`, `x-ms-throttle-scope`, `x-ms-throttle-information`
or `x-ms-resource-unit` headers were ever returned**, on 200s or 429s. Present on every response: `request-id`, `client-request-id`,
`x-ms-ags-diagnostic` (`{"ServerInfo":{"DataCenter":"UK South",…}}`), `OData-Version`, `ETag` (entities), `Preference-Applied: odata.track-changes` (delta).

Confidence: high for "4 is safe, 8 is not"; medium for the exact figure (5–7 not tested separately, to respect the burst budget).

## Conclusion

Keep the concurrency cap at **4 total**, shared by the daemon's sync, outbox and any `$batch` sub-requests (a parallel `$batch`
counts as N concurrent requests). Rely only on `Retry-After`; don't expect `x-ms-throttle-*` headers.
