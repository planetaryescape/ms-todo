# S12: recurrence and completion

Run: 2026-09-24 14:20:18–14:21:57 UTC (a Thursday). Throwaway list `{L}`. Auth header redacted.

## Answer

**Completing a recurring task via `PATCH {"status":"completed"}` does NOT complete that task id.** Graph:
1. keeps the **same id** as the live series, with `status` still `notStarted` and `dueDateTime` **rolled forward** to the next occurrence, and
2. **creates a new task (new id, new `createdDateTime`)** holding the completed occurrence (`status: completed`, old due date, `completedDateTime` set, `recurrence` still present on it).

The PATCH response itself shows `status: "notStarted"` and the rolled due date. `tasks/delta` afterwards returns **both** tasks
(the new completed one and the rolled original). Confidence: high (daily and weekly both behaved this way).

## Other recurrence findings

- **Omitting `range.recurrenceTimeZone` shifts dates.** "S12 daily recurring" (due 24 Sep London, no recurrenceTimeZone) came back with
  due **25 Sep** and `range.startDate` 2026-09-25 (the server filled `recurrenceTimeZone: Europe/London`). "S12 daily recurring due 26th"
  (no recurrenceTimeZone) came back due **27 Sep**, startDate 27th. With `recurrenceTimeZone` set, due was kept.
- A recurrence with **no dueDateTime** gets one: due = `range.startDate` at 00:00 **UTC**, `recurrenceTimeZone: UTC`.
- The server echoes every pattern field (`month:0, dayOfMonth:0, daysOfWeek:[], firstDayOfWeek:"sunday", index:"first"`, `endDate:"0001-01-01"`, `numberOfOccurrences:0`) even when not applicable: treat these as defaults, not data.
- **After a roll, dates are re-based to UTC midnight**: the rolled weekly due became `2026-10-01T00:00:00 UTC` (was sent as London midnight), and
  the completed copy's due/completedDateTime are `2026-09-24T00:00:00 UTC`. `completedDateTime` has no time of day (always 00:00 UTC; BK's real data: 418 of 420 are 00:00Z).
- BK's 67 existing recurring tasks all have `recurrenceTimeZone: UTC` (written by the official apps).
- Deleting a task known to delta → next delta has `{"@removed":{"reason":"deleted"}, "id", "hasAttachments", "isReminderOn"}`.

## Conclusion / design consequences

- The outbox must not verify a completion by reading back `status == completed` on the same id for recurring tasks: success = 200 and
  either `status == completed` **or** (`recurrence != null` and the due date moved forward).
- The cache must expect a brand-new completed task to appear from delta after completing a recurring task (it's the history entry).
  The locally-completed row keeps its id but becomes `notStarted` with a new due: apply the server state; don't treat it as a conflict.
- Always send `recurrenceTimeZone` equal to the due date's zone. Because rolls re-base to UTC midnight, the "round to nearest midnight" read rule from S11 is required.
- Undo of "complete" on a recurring task isn't a simple PATCH back: it would need deleting the new completed copy and restoring the old due date.

## Observed later, and explained (BK, 2026-09-24)

The iOS app put two of the recurring S12 tasks into its own My Day without BK doing it. Graph showed nothing for it: neither v1.0 nor beta has a My Day field on those tasks, and only `lastModifiedDateTime` moved. The cause is the app setting "Show 'Due Today' tasks in My Day", which is on in BK's app (checked read-only in the Mac app): any task due today goes into the app's My Day, and those tasks were due today.
